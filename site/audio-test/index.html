🔊 Audio behaviour test

← back to the Road Guide · Run this from inside the home-screen app so your results are saved where the app can see them.

How to test (about 2 minutes per mode)
  1. Plug the phone into CarPlay/Bluetooth if you can (or use the speaker).
  2. Tap a mode's Play in 20s button. This tap also "unlocks" audio.
  3. Within 20 s: switch to Spotify, start music, then come back to this page and wait (Safari must be in front, as in the car).
  4. When the voice plays, watch the music: does it duck (get quieter), pause then resume, pause and stay paused, or keep playing at full volume?
  5. Tap the matching result button. Results are saved on this phone and summarised at the bottom.

Phone voice for mode E

Mode E sounds robotic with the default voice. Download a better one, free: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → e.g. “Ava (Premium)”, “Zoe (Premium)”, “Evan (Enhanced)”, or British “Serena (Premium)” / “Jamie (Premium)”. Then reload this page and pick it here.

Stay-awake test (for a 5-minute Auto-Lock)

Tap Start, then don’t touch the phone for 6–7 minutes. If the screen is still on, the app can keep it awake on the road.
